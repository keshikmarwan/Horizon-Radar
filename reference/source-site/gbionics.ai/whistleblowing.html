WHISTLEBLOWING

Vai alla versione italiana di questa pagina

Generative Bionics S.r.l. has implemented a whistleblowing reporting channel (the GO-TELL platform) that complies with the requirements of Legislative Decree 24/2023.

The platform allows employees, contractors, and any other party associated with Generative Bionics to report suspected illegal conduct or irregularities, violations of regulations, violations of the 231 Model and the Code of Ethics or gender equality, and in any case violations of company procedures and policies in general, in compliance with Legislative Decree 24/2023.

The management of reports has been entrusted to an external party possessing the necessary qualifications, autonomy, and independence, who is the only one with access to the platform and responsible for receiving and managing reports.

The platform allows reports to be submitted without the need to register, ensuring the confidentiality required by Legislative Decree 24/2023.”
