Graphic Designer

Via Melen 83, 16152 Genoa, Italy Full-time, Permanent

About Us

Generative Bionics is a deep-tech company building humanoid robot platforms to deploy human-centered Physical AI. We design intelligent, capable machines that work alongside people in real-world environments — developed in Genova, Italy.

Role

We are looking for a Graphic Designer to join our communications team. You will produce visual content that communicates our technology and brand, with a strong focus on print-ready materials, digital content, and motion graphics.

Responsibilities

- Design and produce graphics for print and digital communications (brochures, flyers, infographics, social media). - Create layouts and visual assets that maintain brand consistency across all touchpoints. - Edit and produce video and motion graphics content for internal and external communications. - Support the team with presentation design and formatting (PowerPoint). - Manage and organize design assets and templates.

Requirements

- Experience: Minimum 2 years in a graphic design role, agency or in-house. - Technical Skills: Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, and After Effects. - Mindset: Detail-oriented, reliable, and able to follow brand guidelines while contributing fresh ideas.

Valued Extras

- Familiarity with video editing software (preferably Final Cut Pro) - Basic knowledge of 3D computer graphics software (preferably Blender) - Familiarity with tech, industrial, or science communication - Knowledge of English language

We Offer

- The opportunity to work in a cutting-edge deep tech company, in close contact with world-class engineers and researchers. - Informal, fast-paced, and meritocratic environment with quick decision-making and Agile methodologies. - Contractual framework (CCNL Metalmeccanico) commensurate with experience. - Concrete opportunities for professional growth.
