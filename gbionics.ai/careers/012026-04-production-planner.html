Production Planner

Via Melen 83, 16152 Genoa, Italy Full-time, Permanent

About Us

Generative Bionics is a deep-tech company building humanoid robot platforms to deploy human-centered Physical AI. We design intelligent, capable machines that work alongside people in real-world environments — developed in Genova, Italy.

Role

In an environment where design and manufacturing coexist, we need someone to precisely plan the execution. We are looking for a Production Planner to translate R&D designs into concrete operational plans. You will be responsible for the "CAD to Robot" transition: managing technical data via PLM, handling Bills of Materials, and organizing workflows to ensure our humanoids are built on schedule.

Responsibilities

- PLM & BOM Management: Use of the PLM (Product Lifecycle Management) system to manage product lifecycles and revisions. Analyze Engineering Bills of Materials (EBOM) and convert them into Manufacturing Bills of Materials (MBOM). - Planning & Procurement: Generate Purchase Requisitions (RDA) based on project requirements and monitor material availability. - Work Breakdown: Break down the main project into logical sub-activities and Work Orders. - Resource Scheduling: Schedule tasks for assembly technicians based on the availability of warehouse kits, defining assembly priorities. - Process Optimization: Collaborate with engineering to improve product manufacturability and workflow efficiency.

Requirements

- Education: Degree in Management Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or equivalent technical background. - Experience: 2-4 years in production planning or industrialization, preferably in complex assembly or robotics sectors. - Strong knowledge of PLM systems for technical data management. - Proficiency with ERP systems (e.g., MS Business Central) for operations.

Valued Extras

- Analytical mindset, ability to manage deadlines, and proactive attitude.

We Offer

- Environment: An informal, fast-paced, and meritocratic startup context driven by Agile methodologies. - Competitive salary and contract (CCNL Metalmeccanico) in a unique high-tech environment.
