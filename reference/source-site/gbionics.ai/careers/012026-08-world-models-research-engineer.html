World Models Research Engineer

Via Melen 83, 16152 Genoa, Italy Full-time, Permanent

About Us

Generative Bionics is a deep-tech company building humanoid robot platforms to deploy human-centered Physical AI. We design intelligent, capable machines that work alongside people in real-world environments — developed in Genova, Italy.

Role

We are looking for a skilled and enthusiastic AI Research Engineer to help advance autonomy in humanoid robots. In this role, you will develop new World Models that will be used in simulations and in the training and testing of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. Our World Models at Generative Bionics create realistic representations that accurately reflect the physics of the environment, handle dynamic changes in the scene, and work with multiple types of data. This helps improve how humanoid robots are trained and tested in challenging scenarios. You will work closely with a team of dedicated research engineers who specialise in computer vision and robotics. You will also have the chance to apply these models to real-world situations, tackling complex problems in automation and planning.

Responsibilities

- You will be responsible for deploying new data-driven world models to generate training data and create realistic simulations of the environment, subject to physical constraints of the use case: - Training and fine-tuning World models: Leverage the Generative Bionics data collection pipeline to learn new world models that include more than vision data modalities. - Efficiency of representation: Improve the performance of the model by evaluating different 3D representations and semantic mapping. - Deploy, test, and improve: Define new pipelines for rapid re-training and finetuning of models from synthetic and real data. - Interact and learn: Join our cross-functional teams to learn from dynamic control and mechatronics engineers, and integrate their knowledge into the learning and generation frameworks of the world models.

Requirements

- Education: Master’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or equivalent. - Experience: 2-4+ years of experience in computer vision, machine learning or related fields or a Ph.D. in a subject that is related to the topics of the call. - Programming Languages: Proficiency in Python for AI development with production-ready code skills. Strong experience with PyTorch (preferred) or JAX/Tensorflow for training large-scale models. - Multi-modal learning: Proven experience in training models from multi-modal inputs, including high-dimensional visual data (RGB-D, point clouds), natural language instructions, and robot proprioception (joint states, IMU, torque sensors). - 3D vision: Basic knowledge of 3D representations and models from images and other modalities. - Robot deployment experience: Proven experience in deploying and testing ML/CV models on robotic platforms.

Valued Extras

- Training on HPC clusters with experience in multi-node training on GPU clusters. - Knowledge of open-source robotics middleware suites (e.g. YARP, ROS, ROS2). - Publications in top-tier conference venues and journals. - Other programming skills: C/C++.

We Offer

- Technological Challenge: The chance to design models that can understand and interact with the world, for real. - Growth: Work closely with AI and ML experts, expanding your skills in a cross-disciplinary environment, learning everything about building a functional humanoid robot - Environment: An informal, fast-paced, and meritocratic startup context driven by Agile methodologies. - Compensation: Competitive salary and contract commensurate with experience.
